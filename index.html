How to Play

Controls

  • Click: Select a piece, then click destination
  • Drag & Drop: Drag pieces to move them
  • Touch: Tap or drag on mobile

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Escape: Cancel selection
  • Ctrl+N: New game
  • Ctrl+U: Undo
  • Ctrl+F: Flip board
  • Ctrl+S: Settings
  • H: Help
  • Arrow Left/Right: Navigate moves
  • Home/End: First/last move

Special Moves

  • Castling: Move king two squares toward rook
  • En Passant: Capture pawn that just moved two squares
  • Promotion: Pawn reaching the end rank promotes

Features

  • 4 AI difficulty levels (900-2200 ELO)
  • Two-player local mode
  • Time controls (Bullet, Blitz, Rapid, Classical)
  • Move history navigation
  • Opening name recognition
  • Auto-save and resume games
  • PGN import and export
  • Draw offers and resignation
  • 4 board themes
